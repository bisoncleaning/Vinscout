Accident, damage & lien checks

These checks use official or public sources. They do not automatically reveal every accident or lien; some require a paid search, a request to MPI, or checking the vehicle's previous provinces.

1. Accident & insurance damage historyRequest Manitoba vehicle-history information from MPI, which may include previous claims, total losses and vehicle damage.

Open MPI Vehicle History Search Form ↗

2. Write-off / stolen statusMPI can confirm whether a vehicle was an Autopac write-off in Manitoba. This is not a complete Canada-wide accident search.

MPI buyer guidance ↗

3. Manitoba lien searchSearch the Personal Property Registry by the vehicle's VIN/serial number. Fees may apply. Do not treat a skipped search as “no lien.”

Open Teranet Manitoba Personal Property Registry ↗

4. Public damage / auction tracesSearch the exact VIN for old auction pages, salvage listings, photos or ads. A blank result does not prove a clean history.

Search VIN for damage / auction ↗

Important: VINScout cannot directly access private insurance databases or guarantee a nationwide lien result. For out-of-province vehicles, run the appropriate provincial lien searches and obtain a commercial history report if needed.
